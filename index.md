## 📊 What You Will See

The application organizes results into a small number of clear views.

The **student summary** shows the three outcome estimates together with the timing indicator. This is the fastest way to understand a single case.

The **explanation view** breaks the estimate into contributing factors. Factors appear in order of importance, so the top items are the ones worth discussing first. Some factors describe the student's background and preparation. Others describe current academic progress. Others describe engagement and financial or personal circumstances.

The **comparison view** places multiple students side by side. It helps a team sort a long list into a short one.

The **reliability view** summarizes how dependable the estimates are overall, including where the app tends to be less certain. This view is useful when your team needs to justify its review process to leadership.

## 🧠 Understanding the Results

Each estimate reflects patterns found in historical student data. It is a signal, not a verdict. A high dropout estimate means the student's current record resembles records that ended in dropout. It does not mean dropout is certain.

Treat every result as a prompt for a conversation. Students with high dropout estimates are candidates for outreach. Students with mixed or uncertain results may simply need more information gathered before a decision is made.

The timing indicator matters as much as the estimate itself. An early estimate lets your team act during a term. A late estimate may only confirm what staff already suspect.
